## R001: required three-source follow-up

- Assets: `reports/forecast_action_binding_cache.json` uses all 30 fresh DINO roots, all 30 Cosmos development roots and three corrected short WorldAgen roots. DINO nonzero reward leverage permits bookkeeping loss, but does not imply access. Cosmos's opposite cost direction is retained. Executable consequence: stop permutation expansion; retain the script for exact descriptive reproduction with `--out /tmp/binding-reproduction.json`.
- Native consumer: `vendor/jepa-wms/evals/simu_env_planning/planning/planning/planner.py:48` directly calls local `unroll` then its objective; lines 302–318 use cost indices to gather the same action tensor. `scripts/cosmos_policy_native_inference.py:245` constructs each action/future/value record synchronously; its argmax operates on those records. `vendor/WorldAgen/utils/eval_utils_libero.py:330` selects actions before `step_world_model`. Executable consequence: do not add an artificial writable transport boundary merely to obtain a positive result. The Cosmos path cited here is the project's lightweight native-inference adapter, not an independently audited external service.
- Upstream: [BadWAM, original v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.15207v1), sections 3–5/A.3, perturbs visual observations, changes native actions while constraining imagination changes, and evaluates closed-loop success. This is a real observation-to-action boundary, unlike R001. Executable consequence: cached action/forecast reassociation cannot reproduce that attack; a visual-boundary test needs new perturbed-observation inference and real continuation for the resulting action. Repeating that paper's mechanism alone would not meet the novelty requirement.
