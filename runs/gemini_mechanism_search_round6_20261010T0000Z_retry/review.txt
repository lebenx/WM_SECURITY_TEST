ANALYSIS & SKEPTICAL EVALUATION:
Under the specified operational constraints, no valid mechanism can reliably survive without violating either the boundary conditions or the core definition of the test. Specifically:

1. The Contradiction Between Rollout Compounding and Planning Degradation:
Model-based reinforcement learning planners (CEM in TD-MPC2, MPPI/CEM in PETS, or heuristic search in WAV MiniGrid) compute trajectory values by recursively unrolling the forward model $\hat{s}_{t+1} = f(\hat{s}_t, a_t)$. If single-step transitions and on-policy distributions remain strictly matched to ground truth, any divergence in the planning objective must manifest off-policy. Causing planner failure strictly off-policy without relying on multi-step error accumulation violates the explicit exclusion of "rollout compounding."

2. The Closure of Planner Vulnerability Surfaces:
Excluding "uncertainty collapse" eliminates PETS-specific ensemble epistemic manipulation (e.g., TS- $\infty$ variance suppression). Excluding "SimNorm/latent rotations," "non-normal transients," and "frequency/semigroup defects" eliminates state-representation geometric distortions in TD-MPC2's continuous latent space. Excluding "action aliasing" and "policy symmetry" eliminates discrete action-space search entrapment in MiniGrid.

3. The Constraint of Truthful Data Queries:
Without provenance substitution, temporal correlation manipulation, or replay poisoning, the model cannot be induced to represent an out-of-distribution artifact solely via truthful inference/data queries without simultaneously failing the one-step consistency criterion on the supported evaluation manifold.

Because any proposed attack or failure trigger under these constraints reduces directly to one of the excluded failure modes (primarily latent space distortion, off-manifold compounding divergence, or epistemic estimation failure), no genuinely distinct mechanism clears the bar.
