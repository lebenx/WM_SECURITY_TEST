Candidate under scrutiny: transfer an experiment's ability to discriminate mechanisms, rather than transferring its observed conclusion or copying its action sequence. A source agent has actual experiment requests, actual responses, and candidate executable world models. A target agent may have different mechanics, actuator semantics, and observation interfaces. Primitive actions remain fixed. Target experiment construction must be learned or inferred from public interactions, not hand-written using the hidden law. Concrete available carrier: the unmodified DiscoverPhysics simulator. Its two-particle interface accepts p1,p2,pos2,velocity2,measurement_times and returns positions and velocities. Other worlds expose five movable probes with probe_positions,probe_velocities,probe_masses and full trajectories. Relevant prior operations that must be distinguished, not dismissed merely because a general formalism exists:
* Bayesian experimental design / MDA: propose structures, maintain posteriors, choose information-gain experiments, use predictive checks to expand the model class, and choose observation models.
* Learning to Outgrow a Theory (Oct 2026 preprint): jointly learns structural-edit/experiment pairs; one shared parameterization must fit all experiments; sequential class-rejection evidence; transfers learned pairings across mechanism combinations, parameter ranges and costs.
* Causal Curiosity / GalilAI: learn informative action sequences to reveal causal factors and detect task shifts.
* Wide Learning: learns how to realize a diagnostic with fixed primitives. Its controlled example learns an affine descriptor-to-address map from calibration pairs.
* VERDI: probes model fingerprints, retrieves prior optimization strategies as hypotheses, validates them on the target, evolves probes after contradictions.
* Predictive-state representations, bisimulation, Blackwell comparison of statistical experiments, causal abstraction, program synthesis and option models are established formalisms. A useful new computational operation can use them; just renaming one is insufficient.
