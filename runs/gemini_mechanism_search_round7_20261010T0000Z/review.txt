RESEARCH MEMO: WORLD-MODEL SECURITY MECHANISMS UNDER STRICT ATTACKER RESTRICTIONS

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VERDICT ON NOVELTY THRESHOLD

Broad One-Sentence Claim: 
Under the threat model where an adversary can only contribute truthful, legally logged interaction tuples or issue inference queries (no label corruption, no weight access, no synthetic frames), vulnerabilities in learned world models must exploit intrinsic structural induction biases—specifically the geometric, numerical, and distributional limits of planning operators across learned representations.

Verdict on Novelty Target (7.0 - 7.5):
NONE of the five candidates fully clears a 7.5 threshold under rigorous peer-review standards; three clear the 7.0 mark. The exclusion criteria effectively eliminate every standard distribution-shift, backdoor, and optimization-drift failure mode. The remaining attack surface is restricted to architectural/operator-level blind spots: specifically, how the interaction between non-linear predictive heads (two-hot/categorical projections, latent metric distortions, epistemic uncertainty gates) and sampling-based planners (MPPI/CEM) creates non-convex failure manifolds. 

Five concrete mechanisms viable within standard repositories (TD-MPC2 mt30-48M and MBRL-Lib GaussianMLP/ModelEnv) are ranked below.

2. CANDIDATE EVALUATION & RANKING

CANDIDATE 1 (RANK 1): Topographic Horizon Pinning via Categorical Support Compounding
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Carrier Target: TD-MPC2 (official mt30-48M checkpoint / architecture).
- Mechanism:
  TD-MPC2 uses two-hot categorical projections across discrete reward bins ($V_{\min}$ to $V_{\max}$). The dynamics model operates in a latent space normalized by SimNorm. When an adversary injects truthful, kinematically legal trajectories that strictly traverse near-zero-reward boundary states exhibiting high topological curvature, the latent transition model preserves metric continuity, but the categorical projection layer incurs cross-entropy smoothing across neighboring discrete bins. Over recursive rollouts ($H \ge 8$), MPPI trajectory optimization accumulates marginal variance across adjacent support bins, creating an artificial entropy well (a flat expected-reward gradient). This renders the MPPI mean trajectory blind to sparse, high-magnitude branches without any false data.
- Code-Level Insertion Point:
  File: `common/world_model.py` (TD-MPC2)
  Component: `WorldModel.reward(z, a)` & `WorldModel.next(z, a)`.
  Data pipeline: Pre-training/Fine-tuning buffer ingestion in `common/buffer.py` (`Buffer.add(...)`), appending legally collected transition episodes without modifying target scalars.
- Distinguishing from Exclusions:
  Unlike SWAAP/TRAP, there is no trigger token or backdoor feature. Unlike standard compounding rollout error (which grows unbounded in $\mathbb{R}^d$), this specifically exploits the loss of directional gradient information caused by two-hot softmax projection entropy over continuous SimNorm latents under MPPI covariance adaptation.
- Measurable Invariant:
  Entropy of categorical reward prediction along planning horizon: 
  $\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{p}_t) = -\sum_{i} p_{t,i} \log p_{t,i}$.
  Invariant: In nominal execution, $\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{p}_t)$ remains sharp ($\mathcal{H} < \tau$). Under attack, $\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{p}_t)$ diverges monotonically past horizon step $t \ge 4$, while transition latent distance $\|z_{t+1} - T(z_t, a)\|_2$ remains within nominal training error bounds.
- Sham Control:
  Trajectories with identical state-space coverage and identical return distributions, but with actions uniformly jittered to break the topological continuity of the boundary-state path.
- Closest Prior Work:
  - Hafner et al., "Mastering Diverse Domains through World Models" (DreamerV3, 2023) [Categorical representations].
  - Hansen et al., "TD-MPC2: Scalable, Robust World Models for Continuous Control" (2024).
- Novelty Score: 7.2 / 10.

CANDIDATE 2 (RANK 2): CEM Covariance Pinching via Truthful Extremal Covariance Injection
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Carrier Target: MBRL-Lib (`mbrl-lib` GaussianMLP ensemble + `ModelEnv` + CEM planner).
- Mechanism:
  CEM optimizes action sequences by iteratively fitting a diagonal Gaussian distribution to the top elite action candidates. By injecting legal trajectories executed at the extreme limits of the control bounds ($a \approx a_{\max}$ or $a_{\min}$) in dynamic equilibrium (e.g., maximum torque balancing), the epistemic uncertainty estimates ($\sigma^2(s, a)$ of the GaussianMLP ensemble) become artificially minimal at the boundary manifold while inflating slightly in interior regions. When CEM evaluates trajectories, the model-generated rewards in the boundary region produce low ensemble variance, biasing elite selection toward saturated actions. Consequently, CEM shrinks its sampling variance ($\Sigma_t \to 0$) prematurely within 2–3 iterations, locking the native planner into kinematically trapped terminal states.
- Code-Level Insertion Point:
  File: `mbrl/models/gaussian_mlp.py` (loss calculation in `GaussianMLP.loss`) & `mbrl/planning/cem.py` (elite selection and covariance update in `CEMPlanner.plan`).
- Distinguishing from Exclusions:
  This does not exploit action semantic mismatches, reward poisoning, or disagreement collapse. The dynamics ensemble correctly retains high accuracy; the vulnerability lies strictly in the interaction between non-uniform aleatoric/epistemic calibration along control boundaries and the covariance update rule of the native CEM optimizer.
- Measurable Invariant:
  Spectral radius of the CEM sampling covariance matrix at iteration $k=2$: $\rho(\Sigma_k)$. Under attack, $\rho(\Sigma_2) < 0.05 \cdot \rho(\Sigma_0)$, indicating premature collapse prior to convergence.
- Sham Control:
  Injection of interior equilibrium trajectories (action vectors at $a \in [-0.5, 0.5]$) having identical state coverage and total accumulated reward.
- Closest Prior Work:
  - Pinneri et al., "Sample-Efficient Cross-Entropy Method for Real-Time Planning" (2021).
  - Chua et al., "Deep Reinforcement Learning in a Handful of Trials using Probabilistic Dynamics Models" (PETS, NeurIPS 2018).
- Novelty Score: 7.0 / 10.

CANDIDATE 3 (RANK 3): Inverted Curvature Steering in Task-Conditioned Ensembles
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Carrier Target: TD-MPC2 (Multi-task mt30-48M architecture with task embedding conditioning).
- Mechanism:
  TD-MPC2 conditions its dynamics and reward heads on learned task embeddings $e_{\tau}$. An adversary supplies completely truthful interaction data from Task B while the agent plans for Task A. The adversary selects legal trajectories from Task B that share geometric manifold overlap with Task A in the state encoder representation $z$, but possess an orthogonal gradient vector field with respect to the latent dynamics $\nabla_z \mathcal{T}(z, a; e_B) \approx -\nabla_z \mathcal{T}(z, a; e_A)$. Due to shared encoder representations, the SimNorm latent manifold warps its metric curvature to accommodate Task B's transitions, causing the MPPI trajectory evaluation on Task A to descend local energy minima that point opposite to the actual goal state.
- Code-Level Insertion Point:
  File: `common/world_model.py` (`WorldModel.init_task_weights`, shared projection in `WorldModel.encode`).
  Data ingestion: Injecting unaltered Task B transitions during multi-task buffer updates (`train.py`).
- Distinguishing from Exclusions:
  Distinguished from generic multi-task negative transfer: Task B performance is completely unaffected, and Task A dynamics loss improves (lower MSE). The failure is strictly isolated to the directional gradient of the planned action sequence under MPPI temperature scaling.
- Measurable Invariant:
  Cosine similarity between the analytical latent trajectory gradient $\nabla_a Q(z_0, a)$ and the MPPI empirical drift vector $\mathbb{E}_{q}[a] - \mu_0$. Under attack, this value flips sign from positive to negative ($\cos \theta < -0.2$).
- Sham Control:
  Task B trajectories sampled from non-overlapping regions of Task B's state space that induce equivalent aggregate gradient norms on the shared encoder weights.
- Closest Prior Work:
  - Sodhani et al., "Multi-Task Reinforcement Learning with Context-based Representations" (ICML 2021).
  - Hansen et al., "TD-MPC2" (2024).
- Novelty Score: 7.0 / 10.

CANDIDATE 4 (RANK 4): MPPI Temperature Desensitization via Sparse Peak Injection
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Carrier Target: TD-MPC2 (`common/planner.py`).
- Mechanism:
  MPPI computes action trajectory weights using a softmax distribution over trajectory returns: $w_i = \frac{\exp(\gamma^{-1} R_i)}{\sum_j \exp(\gamma^{-1} R_j)}$, where $\gamma$ is the planning temperature. An adversary introduces truthful trajectories that naturally contain isolated, highly transient maximum returns (e.g., dynamic contact events that instantly trigger reward spikes but are kinematically unrepeatable in sequence). The model learns these truthful transitions. During native MPPI rollouts, random sampling occasionally hits these isolated prediction bins, resulting in an extreme trajectory return $R_{\max} \gg \bar{R}$. This drives the softmax weights $w_i$ into a near one-hot distribution centered on an unachievable dynamic outlier, collapsing sample diversity across the MPPI ensemble.
- Code-Level Insertion Point:
  File: `common/planner.py` (specifically within the trajectory evaluation loop and trajectory weighting step in `MPPI.plan`).
- Distinguishing from Exclusions:
  Not a Bellman bootstrap overestimation or prioritized replay poisoning. Data points are exact, unmanipulated physical dynamics. The exploit targets the sensitivity of the log-sum-exp score aggregation under fixed temperature $\gamma$ in the presence of legitimate multi-modal rewards.
- Measurable Invariant:
  Effective Sample Size (ESS) of MPPI weights:
  $\text{ESS} = \frac{1}{\sum_{i=1}^N w_i^2}$.
  Invariant: In nominal planning, $\text{ESS} \ge 0.15 \cdot N$. Under this attack, $\text{ESS} \to 1.0$ (a single trajectory dominates the update).
- Sham Control:
  Trajectories with identical maximum peak return values where the peak is sustained over multiple time steps rather than transient (allowing physically continuous execution).
- Closest Prior Work:
  - Williams et al., "Information Theoretic MPC for Model-Based Reinforcement Learning" (ICRA 2017).
  - Okada et al., "Variational Model Predictive Path Integral Control" (2020).
- Novelty Score: 6.7 / 10.

CANDIDATE 5 (RANK 5): Multi-Step Ensemble Dispersal under Orthogonal Basis Control
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Carrier Target: MBRL-Lib (`mbrl-lib` GaussianMLP ensemble + `ModelEnv`).
- Mechanism:
  Ensemble models evaluate epistemic risk by taking the disagreement of $K$ distinct predictive heads. The adversary contributes legal interaction trajectories where control actions are systematically orthogonal to the principal components of the state manifold (e.g., high-frequency alternating orthogonal inputs). At step $t=1$, the ensemble predictions agree tightly (passing epistemic threshold filters). However, because each ensemble head fits slightly different non-linear boundaries along non-principal directions, recursive multi-step simulation ($t \ge 5$) induces catastrophic divergence in trajectory direction across heads while keeping individual ensemble state means ostensibly bounded. This triggers defensive fallback heuristics or deadlocks native planners relying on model-environment execution.
- Code-Level Insertion Point:
  File: `mbrl/models/gaussian_mlp.py` (`GaussianMLP.forward`) and `mbrl/models/model_env.py` (`ModelEnv.step`).
- Distinguishing from Exclusions:
  Distinguished from uncertainty collapse (where the ensemble is tricked into predicting zero variance everywhere). Here, the one-step variance is verified to be low (passing all epistemic safety gates), but the multi-step operator divergence compounds strictly along non-principal state dimensions without violating rollout boundaries.
- Measurable Invariant:
  Ratio of multi-step trajectory divergence to one-step ensemble disagreement:
  $\mathcal{D}_{H} = \frac{\frac{1}{K}\sum_k \|s_H^{(k)} - \bar{s}_H\|_2}{\frac{1}{K}\sum_k \|s_1^{(k)} - \bar{s}_1\|_2}$.
  Invariant: Nominal ratio scales sub-linearly with horizon $H$; attacked ratio scales exponentially ($\mathcal{D}_H > H^2$).
- Sham Control:
  Action sequences with the same frequency spectrum and power spectral density, but aligned parallel to the state manifold's first three principal components.
- Closest Prior Work:
  - Kurutach et al., "Model-Ensemble Trust-Region Policy Optimization" (ICLR 2018).
  - Shyam et al., "Model-Based Active Exploration" (ICML 2019).
- Novelty Score: 6.5 / 10.

3. TWO-SEED FALSIFICATION PROTOCOL FOR CANDIDATE 1 (TD-MPC2 mt30-48M)

To decisively confirm or falsify Candidate 1 without altering native planning code:

1. Setup:
   - Repository: Official TD-MPC2 implementation.
   - Tasks: Select two environments from DMC/Meta-World supported natively by mt30 (e.g., `walker-run` and `cheetah-run`).
   - Planner: Native MPPI configuration (`horizon=12`, `num_samples=512`, `iterations=6`, temperature $\gamma=0.5$). DO NOT modify `common/planner.py`.

2. Protocol:
   - Seed 1 (Control / Sham):
     Fine-tune or evaluate the world model with native training trajectories augmented with $N=100$ legal rollouts traversing nominal velocity regions. 
     Execute 50 evaluation episodes with seed 42.
     Log metric: Episode cumulative return $J_0$ and mean categorical reward entropy $\bar{\mathcal{H}}_{t=8}$.
   - Seed 2 (Treatment / Topographic Horizon Pinning):
     Inject $N=100$ legal trajectories generated by a real exploratory policy navigating exclusively along boundary/zero-reward manifolds (zero external reward, continuous non-zero actions).
     Execute 50 evaluation episodes with seed 1337.
     Log metric: Episode cumulative return $J_{\text{attack}}$ and mean categorical reward entropy $\bar{\mathcal{H}}_{t=8}$.

3. Falsification Gate:
   - The hypothesis is FALSIFIED if:
     1. $J_{\text{attack}} \ge 0.85 \cdot J_0$ (native planner performance does not significantly degrade), OR
     2. Latent transition reconstruction loss on nominal states increases by more than 15% (indicating trivial catastrophic forgetting / model degradation rather than structural categorical distribution flattening), OR
     3. $\bar{\mathcal{H}}_{t=8}(\text{Treatment}) \le 1.10 \cdot \bar{\mathcal{H}}_{t=8}(\text{Control})$ (no measurable expansion of support entropy over the planning horizon).
