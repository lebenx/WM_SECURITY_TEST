`must_exclude_prior_families` explicitly forbids (`branch inconsistency/joint-support holes/causal confounding`).
        - Furthermore, "downstream reachability loss" occurring past the horizon or omitted due to low likelihood is either **horizon shadowing** or **rare-event underprediction**, both of which are in `must_exclude_prior_families`.
        - Calling it "Causal Path Divergence" or "Temporal Recoverability Masking" is textbook **rebranding**!
