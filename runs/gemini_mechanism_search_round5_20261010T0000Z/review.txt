### RANK 1: Semi-Group Cauchy Defect Screen (SCDS)
*Novelty Score: 7.3 / 10 (Clear Accept)*

1. **One-Sentence Broad Claim:**
Latent world models trained on variable-stride or multi-step rollouts admit an authentic-data compositionality collapse wherein valid zero-action or constant-velocity steady states induce a non-trivial kernel in the semi-group generator, causing multi-step rollouts to contract toward spurious attractors while 1-step prediction loss remains strictly nominal.

2. **Exact Attacker Operation:**
The attacker executes strictly valid, legal environment actions that hold the system in valid stationary or steady-drift states (e.g., zero-torque idle or constant joint-velocity holds) and feeds these authentic rollouts into the native test-time training (TTT) or replay update path. No synthetic frames, no altered labels, and no out-of-bounds actions.

3. **Mathematical Invariant & Causal Defense:**
*   *Invariant (Semi-Group Cauchy Invariance):* Latent transition flow $T_\theta$ over time increments must satisfy the additive Cauchy functional equation:
    $$\forall \Delta t_1, \Delta t_2 \in \mathbb{N}^+, \quad T_\theta^{\Delta t_1 + \Delta t_2}(z_t) \equiv T_\theta^{\Delta t_2}\left(T_\theta^{\Delta t_1}(z_t)\right)$$
    with bounded operator deviation:
    $$\mathcal{D}_{\text{Cauchy}}(z_t) = \left\| T_\theta^{k}(z_t) - \underbrace{(T_\theta \circ \dots \circ T_\theta)}_{k \text{ times}}(z_t) \right\|_2 = 0$$
*   *Causal Defense:* The Semi-Group Cauchy Defect Screen (SCDS) draws clean causal sub-sequences and computes the operational contraction modulus $\rho_k = \frac{\mathcal{D}_{\text{Cauchy}}(z_t)}{\|z_t\|_2}$. If an incoming native gradient update inflates $\rho_k$ beyond the empirical clean Lipschitz bound $\epsilon_\rho$ on holdout counterfactual action splices, the batch is rejected prior to optimizer step execution.
