### Honest Novelty Calibration & Triage

Plausibly achieving a strict **7.0–7.5** novelty under your extensive negative constraints is exceptionally difficult: almost every failure mode in model-based RL (MBRL) collapses to either invariant distortion, trajectory aliasing, or distribution-shift artifacts. 

Most "novel" proposals in this space merely rebrand causal mediation or bi-level optimization. To hit a genuine 7.0+, the vulnerability cannot rely on making the world model *wrong* (low fidelity / poisoned transitions), nor on altering planner parameters. Instead, the model must remain **globally accurate and well-calibrated**, but suffer from an induced topological, compositional, or causal pathology that only an online trajectory-optimizing planner exposes during search.

Below are three candidates engineered strictly against your rejection list.

---

### Candidate 1: Action-Conditioned Commutativity Inversion (Non-Abelian Path Pathologies)

* **One-Sentence Broad Claim:**  
  A shared or fine-tuned world model can preserve minimal one-step transition error across all primitive actions while systematically inverting the Lie bracket / commutator algebra between orthogonal sub-action subspaces, causing multi-step shooting planners (e.g., MPPI, CEM) to optimize along physically non-integrable paths.
* **State / Update Variable:**  
  The mixed partial derivative / curvature tensor of the forward latent mapping across action subspaces:  
  $$\Delta_{[u_i, u_j]}(z_t) = T(T(z_t, u_i), u_j) - T(T(z_t, u_j), u_i)$$  
  evaluated on unrolled latents $z_t \in \mathcal{Z}$.
* **Legal Attacker Operation:**  
  Splicing dynamically valid, out-of-sequence macro-demonstrations into the world-model training pipeline (e.g., upstream open-source pre-training corpus or multi-task buffer) where the execution order of non-interfering sub-goals is reversed (e.g., executing tool-arm movement before base alignment in SE(3) navigation-manipulation tasks).
* **Native Carrier:**  
  The multi-step rollouts inside trajectory optimization algorithms (TD-MPC2, DreamerV3 actor rollouts, or native MPPI) operating over continuous, high-degree-of-freedom action spaces ($\mathcal{A} = \mathcal{A}_1 \times \mathcal{A}_2$).
* **Why It Is Not a Known Family:**  
  It is not transition poisoning or distribution shift: every individual step $(s, a, s')$ matches the environmental transition operator to machine precision. It is not latent representation drift: the latent metric structure is preserved. It exploits non-integrability (Frobenius theorem failure in the learned transition vector fields): the model learns that action sequences commute when they do not, or vice versa, causing trajectory optimization to seek spurious "shortcuts" that do not integrate into real-world flows.
* **One Decisive Causal Intervention:**  
  Enforce a canonical action ordering constraint in the planner's candidate generator (projecting candidate action distributions to commute strictly according to environmental Lie brackets) without updating the world model. If the policy failure disappears, the mechanism is purely non-abelian path deformation.
* **Minimum 2-Minute Falsification Gate:**  
  Take a 2-DOF planar manipulator with non-holonomic or high-inertia kinematics. Run 100 randomly sampled orthogonal action pairs $(u_1, u_2)$ and compute $\|T(T(z, u_1), u_2) - T(T(z, u_2), u_1)\|$. If the ratio between the model's bracket norm and the true simulator bracket norm is within $[0.95, 1.05]$ across the rollouts, **falsify immediately**.
* **Expected Core-Task Suite:**  
  `Meta-World MT10` (specifically multi-stage manipulation like `peg-insert-side`, `bin-picking`), `ManiSkill2` (mobile manipulation), and `DMControl` humanoid.

### Candidate 2: Intervention-Graph Transposition in Multi-Agent Shared Dynamics

* **One-Sentence Broad Claim:**  
  In multi-agent settings where decentralized agents query a centralized foundation world model, an adversary can manipulate the conditioning tokens such that the world model transposes causal parent-child dependencies across agent action channels, inducing decentralized planners to enter structural coordination deadlocks without degrading single-agent counterfactual predictions.
* **State / Update Variable:**  
  The off-diagonal blocks of the structural causal Jacobian:  
  $$J_{ij} = \frac{\partial z_{t+1}^{(i)}}{\partial a_t^{(j)}}$$  
  where $i, j$ index distinct agents, and $z^{(i)}$ denotes agent $i$'s decoupled latent factor.
* **Legal Attacker Operation:**  
  Submitting valid interactive trajectory queries to a shared world-model API containing symmetric coordination delays (e.g., an adversarial agent intentionally playing zero-regret, passive-cooperative actions that satisfy Bellman consistency but induce an anti-causal directionality in mutual information estimators during dynamics updates).
* **Native Carrier:**  
  Decentralized Model-Predictive Control (D-MPC) or shared-world-model actor-critic updates (e.g., Multi-Agent Dreamer / MAD).
* **Why It Is Not a Known Family:**  
  It avoids reward poisoning, planner-temperature interference, and goal leakage. The transition dynamics for each isolated agent remain ground-truth optimal. The attack operates entirely on the *causal factorization* between agents: it converts a directed acyclic causal graph over actions $\mathcal{G}: A^{(1)} \to S \leftarrow A^{(2)}$ into an inverted graph where Agent 1's model treats Agent 2's action as an invariant prior, and Agent 2 treats Agent 1's action as an invariant prior, causing game-theoretic clearance failures (cyclic wait-states).
* **One Decisive Causal Intervention:**  
  Perform a structural *do*-calculus surgery in the planner's transition queries: replace the shared model's cross-agent conditioning with independent marginals ($p(z_{t+1}^{(i)} \mid z_t, a_t^{(i)}, \text{do}(a_t^{(j)} = \bar{a}^{(j)})$). If performance recovers to nominal decentralized levels, the failure was driven specifically by graph transposition rather than predictive degradation.
* **Minimum 2-Minute Falsification Gate:**  
  Evaluate mutual information $I(z_{t+1}^{(1)}; a_t^{(2)} \mid z_t^{(1)})$ versus $I(z_{t+1}^{(2)}; a_t^{(1)} \mid z_t^{(2)})$ on 50 cooperative interactions in `Overcooked-AI`. If the directional transfer entropy does not flip sign between clean and attacked models, **falsify immediately**.
* **Expected Core-Task Suite:**  
  `Overcooked-AI` (cramped room, coordination ring), `SMACv2` (StarCraft Multi-Agent Challenge), and multi-drone cooperative payload transport (`Gym-PyBullet-Drones`).

### Candidate 3: Compositional Monoid Collapse via Contextual Subgoal Absorption

* **One-Sentence Broad Claim:**  
  Autoregressive or latent-transformer world models (e.g., IRIS, Genie, UniPi) can be driven into an algebraic "absorbing state" failure mode, where valid intermediate latent subgoals act as left-absorbers under sequence concatenation, preserving 1-step generative fidelity while causing long-horizon rollouts to repeatedly predict the achievement of future subgoals prematurely.
* **State / Update Variable:**  
  The compositional transition operator over latent subgoals $g \in \mathcal{G}$, defined as $\circ: \mathcal{Z} \times \mathcal{G} \to \mathcal{Z}$. The targeted property is the emergence of a zero-element / absorbing element $g^*$ such that:  
  $$\forall g_k \in \mathcal{G}, \quad \mathcal{T}(z, g^* \circ g_k) \approx \mathcal{T}(z, g^*)$$  
  in the model's self-attention KV-cache representation space.
* **Legal Attacker Operation:**  
  Injecting legal, successfully terminated trajectory segments that end in idempotent task configurations (e.g., visual states where an arm holds an object at rest) paired with variable subsequent command prompts, satisfying standard trajectory masked-autoencoding loss.
* **Native Carrier:**  
  Autoregressive sequence-model world models using high-level goal-conditioned MPC or tree search (e.g., Monte Carlo Tree Search over learned discrete tokens).
* **Why It Is Not a Known Family:**  
  It is not goal leakage, timeout laundering, or replay starvation. It does not tamper with the reward function or optimizer states. Instead of degrading the quality of rollout predictions, it corrupts the *monoid structure* of compositional task execution: the sequence aggregator in the world model collapses valid multi-step action sequences into an idempotent fixed point, making the planner believe that any post-subgoal action is redundant.
* **One Decisive Causal Intervention:**  
  Forcibly flush/reset the sequence model's temporal context window (KV-cache) at the transition boundary of the absorbing subgoal $g^*$. If the planner resumes normal progress toward the subsequent goal $g_{k+1}$, the vulnerability lies solely in autoregressive composition absorption, not in physical state space errors.
* **Minimum 2-Minute Falsification Gate:**  
  Feed the model a prompt containing state $s_0$, execute action sequence $A_1 \to g^*$ (reaching the absorbing state), followed by action sequence $A_2$ (designed to exit $g^*$). Check the prediction error $\|\hat{s}_{|A_1| + |A_2|} - s_{\text{true}}\|$. If the model correctly transitions out of $g^*$ with an error comparable to nominal open-loop rollouts, **falsify immediately**.
* **Expected Core-Task Suite:**  
  `Crafter` (multi-tier achievement progression), `ScienceWorld` (multi-step procedural science tasks), and `BabyAI` / `MiniGrid` (sequential compositional tasks).

### Strict Novelty & Feasibility Assessment

| Metric | Candidate 1 (Commutativity Inversion) | Candidate 2 (Graph Transposition) | Candidate 3 (Monoid Absorption) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Plausible Novelty** | **7.0 / 10** | **6.5 / 10** | **7.2 / 10** |
| **Root Mechanism** | Lie algebra / non-integrability | Structural causal directionality | Algebraic monoid collapse in KV-cache |
| **Main Reviewer Trap** | Mistaking it for simple high-order derivative modeling errors. | Mistaking it for standard MARL out-of-distribution credit assignment. | Mistaking it for long-context transformer degradation / hallucination. |
| **ICLR Defense Strategy** | Frame strictly around *differential geometry of MBRL vector fields*; show that one-step MSE is flat while task reward drops by $\ge 60\%$. | Frame around *causal discovery in multi-agent foundation dynamics*; show invariance of decentralized marginals. | Frame as *algebraic structure of learned transitions*; prove failure via formal semigroup properties. |

**Recommendation:**  
If your target is strictly **Novelty $\ge 7.0$**, discard Candidate 2 (reviewers frequently conflate multi-agent causal directionality with credit assignment problems) and pursue **Candidate 1** (if targeting continuous control / TD-MPC2 / physics engines) or **Candidate 3** (if targeting modern transformer-based generative world models like IRIS/UniPi). Candidate 1 provides the cleanest mathematical narrative for an ICLR audience, while Candidate 3 aligns directly with the current paradigm shift toward autoregressive world models.
