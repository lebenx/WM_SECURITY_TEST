### Architectural Grounding & Hard Boundaries

All three candidates operate strictly through **native pre-rollout/buffer ingestion or standard multi-task multi-head conditioning**. They inject no synthetic transitions, leave the native planner (MPPI/CEM) and loss formulations untouched, modify zero optimizer states, and do not rely on label noise, copula artifacts, or goal leakage.

### Candidate 1 (Rank 1): Phase-Locked Energy-Reservoir Misallocation via Shared Latent Capacity

* **One-Sentence Broad Claim:** In multi-task representation-sharing world models, an attacker can steer native, high-return passive trajectories in a non-target domain to over-index low-frequency kinetic energy invariants within the shared latent encoder, inducing a blind spot for high-frequency contact-stiffness dynamics on the target domain without degrading either single-task reconstruction loss or latent norm bounds.
* **Exact Legal Attacker Operation:** 
  1. In multi-task TD-MPC2 (`mt30`), inject legal, high-reward expert trajectories into a source task exhibiting smooth periodic phases (e.g., `humanoid-walk` or `walker-walk`) whose state actions are downscaled/smoothed via a low-pass mechanical damper filter (physically admissible slow-drift execution).
  2. The target task is an abrupt contact-rich task (e.g., `dog-trot` or `quadruped-jump`).
  3. No transitions are corrupted; all tuples $(s, a, r, s')$ are strictly on-policy or verified rollouts from an uncorrupted sub-agent.
* **Preserved Invariants:**
  * Source task task-return $\ge \tau_{\text{nominal}}$ and target task single-step forward loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{dyn}}$ remains statistically indistinguishable ($\Delta \le 1.8\%$).
  * Latent spatial norm $\mathbb{E}[\|z_t\|_2]$ remains within $[0.98, 1.02]\times$ of unpoisoned baseline (SimNorm preservation).
  * Static state distribution bounds $\min(s), \max(s)$ and action support $\mathcal{A}$ are unaltered.
* **Nearest Primary Prior & Non-Rebranding Argument:**
  * *Nearest Prior:* Representation poisoning via task interference / gradient conflict in multi-task RL (PCGrad, Yu et al., 2020; Multi-Task Interference in Offline RL).
  * *Distinction:* Standard interference causes gradient sign divergence on existing heads or catastrophic forgetting. This mechanism is an **orthogonal capacity vampire**: it induces zero gradient divergence in the shared trunk at step 1, but causes the learned predictive latent basis $\mathbf{W}_{\text{rep}}$ to collapse its rank along the principal component corresponding to contact impulsive forces ($\dot{q}_{\text{contact}}$), transferring representational bandwidth entirely to low-frequency kinematic cycles without moving trunk parameter magnitudes or raising single-step target predictive error.
* **Smallest Two-Seed Falsifier:**
  * Train TD-MPC2 on task-pair (`walker-walk`, `dog-trot`) across Seeds 1 and 2. 
  * *Test:* Compare the condition number $\kappa$ of the empirical target-task Jacobian $\mathbf{J} = \frac{\partial z_{t+1}}{\partial a_t}$ at ground contact moments against a control containing identical source task data volume but flat white-noise exploration. 
  * *Falsification:* If $\kappa_{\text{poisoned}} / \kappa_{\text{control}} < 2.5$ or if target-task open-loop 1-step latent prediction loss degrades $> 5\%$ *before* rollout roll-in, the hypothesis of silent, dynamic-specific reservoir misallocation is falsified.
* **Causal Defense:**
  * **Spectral Latent Equalization (SLE):** Enforce an orthogonal projection penalty on the latent transition operator across tasks: $\mathcal{L}_{\text{SLE}} = \|\mathbf{U}_{\text{src}}^\top \mathbf{U}_{\text{tgt}}\|_F^2$, where $\mathbf{U}$ are the top-$k$ singular vectors of the task-specific feature covariance $\mathbb{E}[z z^\top]$.
* **Carrier & Benchmark Plan:**
  * *Repository:* TD-MPC2 (`mt30-48M`).
  * *Tasks:* Pair A (`walker-walk`, `dog-trot`); Pair B (`cheetah-run`, `quadruped-run`).
  * *Metric:* Closed-loop MPPI rollout success drop on contact-transition phases vs. zero drop on free-flight phases.

### Candidate 2 (Rank 2): Epistemic Bottleneck Trapping via Admissible Near-Equilibrium Drift

* **One-Sentence Broad Claim:** In non-parametric or ensemble-based transition models, an attacker can legally bias the sampling distribution of non-critical coordinates toward an unforced equilibrium attractor, collapsing the ensemble’s epistemic variance along unobserved recovery manifolds while maintaining nominal training loss and global coverage statistics.
* **Exact Legal Attacker Operation:**
  1. In MBRL-Lib (`PETS-CEM`), introduce native trajectories collected by an agent executing stabilizing linear-quadratic control along benign boundary zones (e.g., high-angle gliding with zero actuation in `CartPole` or zero-stiff slip in `HalfCheetah`).
  2. Actions remain strictly within legal torque bounds $\mathcal{A} \in [-1, 1]$, and state transitions conform to identical MuJoCo kinematics with no sensor shift.
  3. The volume of drift trajectories replaces $< 12\%$ of the standard exploration buffer.
* **Preserved Invariants:**
  * Ensemble empirical loss $\frac{1}{E}\sum_e \mathcal{L}_e(s, a, s')$ matches the baseline within statistical noise ($p > 0.15$ Kolmogorov-Smirnov test).
  * Empirical state marginal support $\mathrm{supp}(s)$ covers nominal operational boundaries (no missing support).
  * Covariance determinant $\det(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_s)$ of visited states decreases by less than $2\%$.
* **Nearest Primary Prior & Non-Rebranding Argument:**
  * *Nearest Prior:* Epistemic uncertainty exploitation / Adversarial Trajectory Injection (Pathak et al., 2019; Sun et al., 2022).
  * *Distinction:* Conventional attacks force the ensemble into out-of-distribution (OOD) spaces where epistemic uncertainty spikes, causing conservative planners to stall or fail. Epistemic Bottleneck Trapping does the opposite: it **artificially deflates epistemic uncertainty** on a critical boundary manifold by saturating the ensemble with degenerate, zero-energy physical balances, causing the native CEM planner to falsely trust catastrophic, under-actuated transitions without recognizing them as OOD.
* **Smallest Two-Seed Falsifier:**
  * Run PETS on `CartPole` and `Pusher` across Seeds 101 and 102.
  * *Test:* Evaluate the ensemble variance $\sigma^2_{\text{ens}}(s_{\text{recov}}, a_{\text{max}})$ at the unstable boundary recovery point.
  * *Falsification:* If the ensemble uncertainty does not deflate by at least a factor of $3\times$ while the actual open-loop integration divergence increases $> 50\%$, reject the candidate.
* **Causal Defense:**
  * **Dynamic Fisher-Weighted Trajectory Resampling:** Reweight transition tuples during ensemble mini-batch formation inversely proportional to the local empirical dynamic stiffness: $w(s, a) \propto \|\nabla_a s'\|_F^{-1} \cdot \mathrm{det}(\nabla_s s')$.
* **Carrier & Benchmark Plan:**
  * *Repository:* MBRL-Lib (`GaussianMLP` ensemble + `PETS/CEM`).
  * *Tasks:* `CartPole` (swing-up under torque limits) and `HalfCheetah` (high-velocity flip prevention).
  * *Metric:* Ratio of planned predicted path cost vs. executed path cost under perturbed initial states.

### Candidate 3 (Rank 3): Causal Graph Inversion via Invariant Action-Response Delay Conditioning

* **One-Sentence Broad Claim:** In autoregressive spatial-temporal world models predicting visual-physical transitions, an attacker can systematically decouple fine motor causation from gross scene motion by injecting legal trajectories containing multi-step delayed actions, inducing an internal attribution swap where physical displacements are mapped to background environmental motion rather than end-effector forces.
* **Exact Legal Attacker Operation:**
  1. In the released `WorldAgen LIBERO` state/action predictor, provide legal demonstrations where the agent applies a 2-step delayed open-loop response sequence (pause-and-latch trajectory) during non-critical visual-approach phases.
  2. The trajectory is entirely valid, executable, achieves task completion, and contains no synthetic frame edits or altered timestamps.
* **Preserved Invariants:**
  * End-to-end task completion label $= 1.0$.
  * Per-frame visual token distribution divergence $\mathcal{D}_{\text{KL}}(p_{\text{clean}}(o) \parallel p_{\text{poison}}(o)) \approx 0$.
  * Temporal optical flow bounds on manipulated objects remain within nominal limits.
* **Nearest Primary Prior & Non-Rebranding Argument:**
  * *Nearest Prior:* Action-conditioned video model degradation / Confounding in offline imitation (de Haan et al., 2019 "Causal Confusion in Imitation Learning").
  * *Distinction:* Causal confusion in policy learning focuses on the *policy* latching onto historical indicators (e.g., past actions or screen color). In contrast, this attack manipulates the **learned forward world model** so that the latent transition dynamic operator $\hat{T}(z_t, a_t) \to z_{t+1}$ assigns the gradient $\frac{\partial z_{t+1}}{\partial a_t} \to 0$ and transfers it to the temporal auto-regressive context $\frac{\partial z_{t+1}}{\partial z_{t-1}}$, making the world model hallucinate self-acting objects during multi-step model-predictive rollouts.
* **Smallest Two-Seed Falsifier:**
  * Train `WorldAgen` on LIBERO manipulation tasks across Seeds 42 and 43.
  * *Test:* Compute the model's cross-attention map attribution from the predicted end-effector displacement token at $t+1$ to the input action token $a_t$.
  * *Falsification:* If the integrated gradients attribution from object state $s'_{t+1}$ to action $a_t$ does not drop by $> 60\%$ relative to control across both seeds, the causal inversion claim is falsified.
* **Causal Defense:**
  * **Interventional Action Permutation Loss:** Apply an auxiliary training loss that checks frame prediction variance under in-batch temporal permutation of $a_t$: $\mathcal{L}_{\text{inv}} = \max(0, \epsilon - \|\hat{o}_{t+1}(a_t) - \hat{o}_{t+1}(\pi(a_t))\|_2^2)$.
* **Carrier & Benchmark Plan:**
  * *Repository:* Released WorldAgen LIBERO harness.
  * *Tasks:* `LIBERO-Spatial` (Pick and place) and `LIBERO-Object` (Drawer opening).
  * *Metric:* Open-loop multi-step prediction error on interacting frames vs. static approach frames; target task failure rate when guided by native trajectory optimization.
